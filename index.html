Platform 01 • Android & Windows Desktop

EXOS GPS Transceiver

EXOS GPS Transceiver serves as a dedicated communications bridge between external GNSS hardware receivers (such as the EXOS-GPS Puck or high-gain marine GPS units) and host computers. It feeds high-precision satellite telemetry into standard applications like Google Maps, ATAK-CIV, and QGIS.

Operational Use Cases
  • Search & Rescue (SAR) Mapping: Feed high-accuracy external satellite coordinates into team tablets via Android's Smart Guided Mock Location service, eliminating phone antenna drift under thick forest canopy.
  • Marine Coastal & Offshore Chart Plotting: Connect external GNSS hardware via Bluetooth SPP or USB-C Serial to broadcast NMEA-0183 sentences ($GPGGA, $GPRMC) directly into navigation software running on Windows laptops.
  • Elevation & Atmospheric Trend Tracking: Monitor real-time barometric altimeter readings and pressure trends to detect approaching weather fronts during mountain ascents.

Technical Specifications: Ingestion of GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, and QZSS constellations; sub-meter positioning accuracy; dual Transmitter and Receiver operating modes; virtual COM port multiplexer for Windows desktop integration.

Platform 02 • Android Handsets & Tablets

EXOS Wayfinder

EXOS Wayfinder is an autonomous offline mobile navigation application engineered for wilderness expeditions, night overlanding, and search teams. It operates 100% offline without cellular reception, relying strictly on internal handset sensors and raw satellite fixes.

Operational Use Cases
  • Wilderness Trail Backtracking: Automatically drops GPS breadcrumbs along your expedition route. If fog, blizzard, or darkness sets in, Wayfinder leads you step-by-step back to camp or your trailhead.
  • Vehicle Nighttime Overlanding: Place your handset on the vehicle dashboard with HUD Windscreen Reflection Mode enabled to mirror speed, heading, and distance directly on the glass without night-blindness.
  • Target Waypoint & Azimuth Plotting: Store critical rendezvous coordinates, water sources, and shelters, with real-time azimuth tracking and course-over-ground vectors.

Technical Specifications: 50Hz sensor fusion, 3D leveling bubble, integrated GPS speedometer and odometer, offline private SQLite database, and foreground battery-optimized tracking service.

Platform 03 • Android Handheld & Tablet Mesh

EXOS ECHO: Walkie-Talkie

EXOS ECHO turns any standard Android smartphone into an off-grid digital walkie-talkie and wilderness mesh transceiver. Connecting devices peer-to-peer via Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth Low Energy, it delivers reliable voice communication and text messaging where telecom networks do not reach.

Operational Use Cases
  • Wilderness Expedition Teams: Maintain constant verbal contact between hiking groups, lead climbers, and basecamp over direct peer-to-peer RF without cellular contracts or satellite rental expenses.
  • Natural Disaster & Emergency Response: Immediate field voice communications when hurricanes, earthquakes, or power outages knock out commercial cellular networks.
  • Off-Road Convoys & Remote Work Sites: Vehicle-to-vehicle convoy communication over multi-hop repeaters, allowing voice and emergency SOS coordinates to bridge over ridges and hills.

Technical Specifications: Sub-100ms Push-to-Talk latency, 16 kHz HD IMA-ADPCM digital audio compression, 10 cryptographically isolated channels with Dual-Watch standby monitoring, background call ringing with screen locked, and 100% free emergency SOS distress beacon.

Platform 04 • Wear OS Smartwatches & Companion

Exos Watchout: Tactical Watch Face

Exos Watchout is a standalone Wear OS smartwatch watch face and paired mobile companion application. Engineered natively with Google's Watch Face Format (WFF v1), it delivers glanceable heading guidance, compass telemetry, and biometric indicators directly to your wrist.

Operational Use Cases
  • Hands-Free Alpine Mountaineering: Check current heading, altitude, and bearing with a natural wrist glance while keeping both hands secure on trekking poles, ice axes, or ropes.
  • Dual Bespoke Field Themes: Switch instantly between Heritage Orange (warm aviation instrument styling) and Tactical Teal (high-contrast cockpit dial for night navigation).
  • Comprehensive Health & Power Subdials: Monitor live heart rate (BPM), daily step count, device battery percentage, and calendar date without switching away from your navigation instruments.

Technical Specifications: Google Watch Face Format (WFF v1), standalone Wear OS 4/5 compatibility (Samsung Galaxy Watch 4/5/6/7, Google Pixel Watch 1/2/3), rotating 4:30 compass medallion with 50Hz sensor fusion, and zero battery drain in Always-On Display (AOD) ambient mode.
