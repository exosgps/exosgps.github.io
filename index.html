PRECISION NAVIGATION

Tactical Compass & 3D Spirit Leveling

Engineered for mountaineers and tactical explorers navigating rugged topography. Includes a fluid 50Hz dynamic azimuth compass dial, real-time spirit leveling bubble, and instantaneous magnetic declination calculations.

  • Dual-sensor sensor fusion (accelerometer, magnetometer + external EXOS GNSS).
  • Line-of-Sight (LOS) Distance-To-Go (DTG) destination telemetry badge.
COCKPIT OPTIMIZATION

Night Windscreen Speedometer HUD

Mount your device on your vehicle dashboard facing the windshield. With HUD Invert Mode, the speedometer, heading, and distance targets mirror flawlessly against the glass for zero-distraction night overlanding.

  • Instantaneous horizontal inversion switch for windscreen projection.
  • Smooth velocity hold filter with high-contrast tactical night green display.
SURVIVAL & RECOVERY

Breadcrumb Backtrack & Guardian SOS

Never lose your bearings in whiteouts or uncharted canyons. Backtrack drops high-fidelity breadcrumbs along your expedition, while Guardian SOS beacons broadcast exact coordinates and rescue codes.

  • Autonomous trail retracing without cellular or map tile dependencies.
  • Emergency Guardian SOS strobe and coordinate broadcasting.
