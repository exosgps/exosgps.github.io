Platform 01 • GNSS Bridge & Telemetry Station

EXOS GPS Transceiver

EXOS GPS Transceiver is a high-precision GNSS positioning and Bluetooth serial bridge suite engineered by RiTym Technologies. It transforms your Android device into a standalone satellite GPS transmitter or external Bluetooth receiver, feeding standard NMEA coordinates into third-party mapping software.

  • ✓
    Dual Transmitter & Receiver Architecture: In Transmitter mode, the app accesses raw hardware GNSS fixes and broadcasts standard NMEA 0183 sentences ($GPRMC, $GPGGA, $GPVTG, $GPHDT) over Bluetooth Classic SPP (RFCOMM UUID 00001101) and BLE NUS. In Receiver mode, it pairs with external Bluetooth GPS hardware, marine chartplotters, or ESP32 stations.
  • ✓
    Smart Guided Android Mock Location Provider: Ingests external satellite fixes and feeds them directly into Android's system location provider. This enables third-party navigation apps (Google Maps, OsmAnd, Waze, ATAK, EXOS Wayfinder) to operate with high-precision external GPS positioning.
  • ✓
    Windows PC & Laptop Serial Bridge: Connects to Windows 10/11 laptops over standard Bluetooth Serial COM ports, providing full compatibility with GIS and marine navigation software including OpenCPN, QGIS, ArcGIS, and Google Earth Pro.
  • ✓
    Live GNSS Constellation & NMEA Diagnostics: Real-time satellite monitor tracking locked satellites, satellites in view, and SNR signal levels, alongside a live scrolling NMEA sentence diagnostic terminal.
  • ✓
    100% Offline & Private: Zero internet or cloud servers required. All satellite telemetry and Bluetooth communication executes strictly on-device with zero tracking or telemetry collection.

Platform 02 • Standalone Backcountry Navigation

EXOS Wayfinder

A standalone offline navigation instrument engineered for zero-connectivity wilderness environments. With locally rendered vector topographic maps, military grid referencing, and reverse backtrack tracking, EXOS Wayfinder guides you safely without reliance on cellular coverage.

  • ✓
    Offline Topographic Vector Mapping: Offline vector contours, ridgelines, and terrain profiles rendered directly on-device using local MapLibre engines with zero cellular data consumption.
  • ✓
    Military Grid & Multi-Format Coordinates: Instant toggling between MGRS (Military Grid Reference System), UTM, Decimal Degrees, and DD°MM'SS" for precise positioning.
  • ✓
    Trail Breadcrumbs & Reverse Backtrack: Continuous local SQLite GPS breadcrumb recording with turn-by-turn reverse backtrack steering to safely retrace routes in zero-visibility conditions.
  • ✓
    Dynamic Cockpit HUD: High-contrast expedition instrumentation displaying Course-Over-Ground (COG), Doppler ground speed, barometric altitude, and waypoint distance vectors.
  • ✓
    Peer Radar (Short-Range BLE Beacons): Direct device-to-device proximity tracking over Bluetooth Low Energy radio mesh without cellular or internet infrastructure.

Platform 03 • Off-Grid Mesh Walkie-Talkie

EXOS ECHO

An off-grid digital walkie-talkie and wilderness mesh transceiver connecting Android devices directly over Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth Low Energy. EXOS ECHO delivers low-latency digital voice and text communications where cellular coverage does not exist.

  • ✓
    Sub-100ms Push-to-Talk (PTT) Digital Voice: Half-duplex digital voice communications utilizing high-efficiency audio codecs optimized for clear intelligibility in noisy outdoor environments.
  • ✓
    Peer-to-Peer Wi-Fi Direct & BLE Mesh: Self-forming, decentralized radio mesh routing packets across multiple hops to extend communication range beyond line-of-sight.
  • ✓
    Offline Encrypted P2P Messaging: Local text messaging and waypoint broadcast channel with message delivery acknowledgments across the mesh.
  • ✓
    Emergency Distress Beacon: One-tap emergency broadcast that floods the local mesh network with distress telemetry and coordinates to alert all team devices.

Platform 04 • Wear OS Compass Watch Face & Companion

Exos Watchout: Compass Watch Face

Exos Watchout pairs a high-altitude Swiss chronograph aesthetic with an expedition-grade 3D outdoor compass engine for Wear OS smartwatches. Designed for clean wrist orientation, it combines an official Google Watch Face Format (WFF v1) dial with seamless companion sensor telemetry.

  • ✓
    Dual High-Contrast Color Themes: Toggle seamlessly between Obsidian Teal (deep obsidian backdrop with luminous cyan indices) and Heritage Orange (warm outdoor lume and amber chronograph markers).
  • ✓
    Expedition-Grade 3D Compass Companion: 50Hz sensor-fused tilt-compensated magnetometer orientation with True North and Magnetic North tracking, 3-digit numeric azimuth readouts, and GPS ground speed telemetry (km/h).
  • ✓
    Live Subdial Telemetry: Battery Gauge (9 o'clock subdial), Live Heart Rate (3 o'clock subdial), Daily Step Progress (6 o'clock subdial), and calendar date display.
  • ✓
    Battery Conservative Architecture: Built using Google's native Watch Face Format (WFF v1) for Wear OS 4 and 5. Ultra-efficient ambient Always-On Display with an On-Pixel Ratio (OPR) below 2.5% for all-day endurance.
