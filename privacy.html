RITYM Technologies and EXOS Systems operate strictly around on-device data sovereignty. 4. EXOS FM Radio Tuner (com.exos.fmradio) Streaming, Recording & Support Logs

For EXOS FM Radio Tuner (com.exos.fmradio):

  • No Account & No Tracking: EXOS FM Radio Tuner requires no sign-in and collects zero personal information, listening history, or analytics.
  • Third-Party Public Streams: When you play a live internet radio station or podcast, your device connects directly to the third-party broadcaster's public stream server, RSS feed, or the open-source Radio-Browser directory. Those third-party endpoints operate under their own privacy policies and receive standard network headers (such as IP address) solely to deliver audio.
  • Local Recordings & No Microphone Access: EXOS FM Radio Tuner does not request microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) permission. Any broadcasts you choose to record are captured directly from the active audio stream and saved exclusively to local storage on your device, where you can play, share, or delete them at any time.
  • Optional Hardware & Permissions: Bluetooth and USB-C permissions are requested strictly on demand when you choose to connect an optional EXOS FM receiver. No location data is collected.
  • Voluntary Diagnostic Support Logs: Local diagnostic logs remain on your device and are only shared if you explicitly tap Send Logs in Settings, which opens your own email client addressed to assist@ritym.com.
  • In-App Updates: Uses the official Google Play In-App Updates API solely to check whether a newer app version is available on Google Play.

5. Wear OS Health & Sensor Data Compliance

For EXOS Watchout (exos.ritym.watchout.watchface and exos.ritym.watchout):

  • Heart rate telemetry collected via BODY_SENSORS is strictly utilized for immediate visual rendering on the active dial complication.
  • EXOS Watchout does not maintain a historical health database, does not sync biometric data to Google Fit or Health Connect, and does not transmit biometric telemetry over any network interface.
  • Ambient Always-On Display (AOD) adheres strictly to Google's Watch Face Format (WFF v1) specifications, maintaining an On-Pixel Ratio (OPR) below 2.5% with zero background battery drain.

6. Microsoft Windows Desktop & Serial Bridge Policy

For the EXOS GPS Windows Client and Virtual COM Sensor Provider:

  • The Windows desktop client operates purely as a local communications bridge between Bluetooth serial COM ports and the Windows Location Sensor Provider API.
  • The desktop software does not collect diagnostic crash logs, telemetry metrics, or machine identifiers.
  • Zero network sockets are opened to external internet hosts.

9. User Rights, Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

Because all data generated by our applications (such as saved waypoint markers, station favorites, local broadcast recordings, and trail backtrack history) resides exclusively in local on-device storage, you maintain complete data autonomy:

  • Immediate Data Deletion: You can delete saved recordings, station favorites, diagnostic logs, or local databases at any time directly inside each application's settings or library.
  • Application Uninstall: Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all application databases and configuration files stored in Android private app storage.
  • No Account Deletion Delay: Because our applications require no user account registration, no remote personal records exist on any cloud server to request deletion for.

10. Child Safety & COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance

Our applications are designed for general audiences (13+), outdoor exploration, navigational assistance, and radio listening.
