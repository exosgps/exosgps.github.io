Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 • Version 2.2
Core Privacy Guarantee: All EXOS applications (EXOS Transceiver, EXOS Wayfinder, EXOS ECHO, and Exos Watchout) operate on a strict offline-first architecture. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your location, telemetry, voice data, or sensor metrics. No user registration is required, and no advertising SDKs are installed.

1. Scope & Covered Applications

This policy applies to all software and hardware components published by RiTym Technologies under the EXOS product ecosystem:

  • EXOS GPS Transceiver: Package ID com.ritym.exosgps (Android) and Windows Desktop COM client.
  • EXOS Wayfinder: Package ID com.exos.wayfinder (Android).
  • EXOS ECHO: Mesh Walkie-Talkie & Messaging system (Android).
  • Exos Watchout: Watch Face package exos.ritym.watchout.watchface and Companion app exos.ritym.watchout (Wear OS & Android).
  • EXOS Hardware Stations: ESP32-S3 and u-blox GNSS firmware instruments.

2. Location Data & Mock Location Service

Our applications require access to Fine and Coarse location permissions exclusively to perform local instrumentation:

  • EXOS Transceiver: Reads hardware GNSS positioning to broadcast standard NMEA 0183 sentences over local Bluetooth SPP. When operating in receiver mode, it utilizes the Android Mock Location service strictly to forward external Bluetooth GPS data to the operating system.
  • EXOS Wayfinder: Calculates real-time coordinates, MGRS/UTM grids, altitude, and course-over-ground. Saves local waypoint and backtrack breadcrumb logs exclusively to local SQLite databases.
  • Exos Watchout: Reads ground speed and coordinate azimuth locally for 3D compass orientation.
  • Zero Cloud Transmissions: Location coordinates are never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

3. Bluetooth & Nearby Device Communications

Bluetooth Classic (RFCOMM/SPP) and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) permissions are utilized strictly for local peer-to-peer hardware communication:

  • Pairing with external GNSS hardware receivers, marine chartplotters, and ESP32 stations.
  • Transmitting raw NMEA sentences between paired field tablets and laptops.
  • Local peer beacon proximity tracking (Peer Radar) without cellular or internet connectivity.

4. Microphone & Audio Communications (EXOS ECHO)

EXOS ECHO requests microphone access solely when the user presses the Push-to-Talk (PTT) interface. Audio packets are digitized into low-latency frames and transmitted directly to connected mesh peers via local Wi-Fi Direct or BLE. Audio streams are never recorded to disk, analyzed, or sent to cloud servers.

5. Body Sensors & Health Metrics (Exos Watchout)

On Wear OS smartwatches, Exos Watchout requests the BODY_SENSORS permission solely to read your heart rate sensor for the 3 o'clock subdial display. Heart rate readings are processed ephemerally in RAM and are never stored or transmitted off the watch.

6. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

All software upgrades (such as Pro Lifetime licenses) are processed exclusively through official Google Play In-App Billing. RiTym Technologies does not collect, receive, or store your credit card information, bank details, or billing addresses.
