Privacy Policy

Published by RiTym Technologies • Applicable to: EXOS Wayfinder, EXOS GPS Transceiver, and EXOS ECHO
Core Privacy Commitment: All EXOS applications are designed with an offline-first, zero-telemetry architecture. Your real-time GPS coordinates, compass headings, breadcrumb trails, voice packets, and text messages are processed strictly on-device or transmitted directly peer-to-peer over local RF (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi Direct). We do not operate user-tracking servers, we do not profile users, and we never monetize or sell personal data.

1. Introduction & Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy governs the suite of field applications developed and operated by RiTym Technologies under the EXOS brand:

  • EXOS Wayfinder: Wear OS Watch Face & Android Mobile Companion (Package: exos.wayfinder / exos.ritym.watchout.watchface)
  • EXOS GPS Transceiver: GNSS Telemetry & Mock Location Bridge (Package: com.ritym.exosgps / exos.ritym.transceiver)
  • EXOS ECHO: Peer-to-Peer Walkie-Talkie & Mesh Communicator (Package: exos.echo.mesh)

2. Permissions & Data Usage by Application

A. EXOS Wayfinder (Wear OS & Mobile Companion)

Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Used strictly on-device to compute real-time bearing, speed, and emergency breadcrumb trail backtracking. Location data is stored ephemerally in a local on-device database and is never transmitted over the internet.
Foreground Service - Location (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): Allows continuous breadcrumb recording during active wilderness hikes when the screen is turned off.
Device Sensors (Compass & Gyroscope): Used on-device to drive the 50Hz dynamic heading compass and 3D spirit level dial.
Health Data (Wear OS BPM & Steps): Read locally from Wear OS health provider complications to display on watch face subdials. No biometric data is stored or transferred.

B. EXOS GPS Transceiver

Precise Location & Mock Location: Used to stream raw NMEA-0183 sentences ($GPGGA, $GPRMC) over Bluetooth Classic SPP or USB Serial to external navigation tablets or chartplotters, and to inject high-accuracy external GNSS fixes into Android's Smart Guided Mock Location provider.
Foreground Service - Connected Device: Maintains an uninterrupted RFCOMM serial stream to paired marine displays or Windows PCs while running in the background.

C. EXOS ECHO (Walkie-Talkie)

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively while the user actively presses the Push-to-Talk (PTT) button to encode real-time digital voice packets for peer-to-peer transmission. Audio is never recorded to disk or uploaded to cloud servers.
Nearby Devices & Wi-Fi Direct: Used to discover neighboring teammates and establish direct ad-hoc peer-to-peer Wi-Fi sockets without internet routing.
Local Notifications: Provides background ringing and incoming transmission alerts when fellow team members transmit on the active frequency channel.

3. Data Storage & User Control

All waypoints, tracks, channel presets, and chat logs are stored strictly within the private sandboxed storage of your physical device. You have absolute control to clear, purge, or export this data at any time via in-app settings.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

We do not integrate commercial third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics. Basic crash telemetry is handled strictly through Google Play Console's standard anonymized vitals.
