Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Smart Guided Mock Location in EXOS GPS Transceiver?
Android includes a developer feature called 'Select mock location app.' When enabled with EXOS GPS Transceiver, external satellite coordinates ingested via Bluetooth from the EXOS-GPS Puck or high-gain marine GPS are injected directly into Android's core location framework. This allows third-party navigation apps (Google Maps, Waze, ATAK, OsmAnd) to navigate with sub-meter external precision.
2. How does EXOS Wayfinder trail backtracking work offline?
EXOS Wayfinder records GPS breadcrumbs locally into an on-device sandboxed SQLite database as you travel. When you engage Backtrack mode, it calculates real-time azimuth and distance to each recorded point in reverse sequence, leading you safely back through fog, blizzards, or heavy canopy without needing mobile internet or cloud maps.
3. Does EXOS ECHO require cellular towers or mobile data?
No. EXOS ECHO establishes direct peer-to-peer Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth Low Energy RF links between handsets. It works 100% off-grid in remote canyons, open water, dense forest, or during total infrastructure blackouts with sub-100ms Push-to-Talk audio and encrypted text.
4. How do I install Exos Watchout on my Samsung Galaxy Watch?
Ensure your watch is paired with your Android phone via the Galaxy Wearable app. Once enrolled in our Google Play testing track, download Exos Watchout from the Wear OS Google Play Store on your watch. After installation, touch and hold your current watch face, swipe right to the '+' icon, and select Exos Watchout under Downloaded.
