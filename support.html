Technical Support & Engineering FAQ

Operational guides and engineering assistance for the EXOS suite and RiTym ESP32 hardware.

1. EXOS GPS Transceiver Configuration

How do I configure Android Mock Location for EXOS Transceiver?
1. Open your Android device Settings > About Phone.
2. Tap Build Number seven times to enable Developer Options.
3. Navigate to Settings > System > Developer Options.
4. Scroll to Select mock location app and select EXOS GPS.
5. Return to EXOS Transceiver, enable Receiver Mode, and pair with your external GNSS beacon. All mapping apps will now consume external coordinates.
How do I stream GPS coordinates to Windows 10/11 laptops?
Pair your Android smartphone running EXOS Transceiver in Transmitter Mode with your Windows PC via Bluetooth. Windows will establish a standard incoming Serial Port (e.g. COM3 or COM4). In your GIS software (OpenCPN, QGIS, ArcGIS), select this COM port at 9600 or 38400 baud to ingest live NMEA sentences.

2. Exos Watchout Wear OS Assistance

How do I switch between Obsidian Teal and Heritage Orange themes?
Touch and hold (long-press) the watch face dial > tap Customize (pencil icon) > swipe or turn the physical bezel to toggle between Obsidian Teal and Heritage Orange > press the top physical button to save.
How do I launch the full-screen 3D Compass from the watch face?
Tap the miniature compass medallion located at the 4:30 position on the watch face to jump directly into the full-screen 50Hz sensor-fused 3D compass telemetry screen.
