4. Technical FAQ & Operational Guides

How do I configure Android Mock Location for EXOS Transceiver?
1. Open your Android device Settings > About Phone.
2. Tap Build Number seven times to enable Developer Options.
3. Navigate to Settings > System > Developer Options.
4. Scroll to Select mock location app and select EXOS GPS.
5. Return to EXOS Transceiver, enable Receiver Mode, and pair with your external GNSS beacon. All mapping apps (Google Maps, Waze, etc.) will now ingest external coordinates seamlessly.
How do I stream GPS coordinates to Windows 10/11 laptops?
Pair your Android smartphone running EXOS Transceiver in Transmitter Mode with your Windows PC via Bluetooth. Windows will establish a standard incoming Serial Port (e.g. COM3 or COM4). In your navigation or GIS software (OpenCPN, QGIS, ArcGIS), select this COM port at 9600 or 38400 baud to ingest live NMEA sentences.
